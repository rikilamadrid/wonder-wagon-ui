# Typography

Three roles, no web fonts. **Working** (system sans) is the default and everything read
for more than a moment. **Engraved** (Georgia, Iowan Old Style) is for names and claims,
never a paragraph over three lines; italic is the world voice. **Plate** (mono, 800,
uppercase, tracked) is for labels on objects, never a sentence.

The seven-step scale was approved for v0.1 and may be refined with implementation
evidence before 1.0. Plate sits outside the scale at 11px.
