# Accessibility

Character never buys out quality. What is measured mechanically, on every change:

- **Contrast** — every declared pairing, 4.5:1 text and 3:1 non-text, computed with the
  WCAG 2.x formula from the token source. The record is `CONTRAST.md` in the tokens
  package and `CONTRACT.md` in the themes package.
- **axe** — every story in this Storybook runs in Chromium through the Vitest addon with
  the a11y addon in `error` mode; a violation fails the run.
- **Keyboard** — play functions tab to controls, press Space and Enter, and assert
  `aria-checked`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, focus and accessible names.
- **Focus** — the environment-scoped ring, verified in the Focus foundation and in the
  dark-panel story.
- **Target size** — every control is at least 44px tall by token.
- **Reduced motion** — durations collapse to 1ms by token; state stays in text.

Still by hand, per release: a screen-reader pass, 200% zoom at 320px, and
`forced-colors`, where the depth stacks vanish and the borders must carry the shape.
