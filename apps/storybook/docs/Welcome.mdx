# Maker 047's bench

This is the workshop where the Wonder Wagon tools are made, and the reference objects
they are measured against. Wonder Wagon UI is **tokens-first**: `@wonder-wagon/tokens`
is the contract every product in the family builds on — Pathfinder's Starlight docs,
Forge's Node CLI, Lorekeeper's kit — and this React library is one consumer of it.

Every story sits in a **specimen tray** on the bench. The toolbar throws three levers:
the **environment** (day, night, auto), the **product theme** (the bench itself, or one
of the four products), and the **ground** behind the specimen. Nothing in a component
knows which product it is in; the theme sets six variables and everything follows.

## The one rule

Important things are objects with material, edge and thickness. Supporting things are
quiet paper and plain type. If a component appears more than three times on one
screen, it is quiet. Objects are singular.

<ObjectQuietSpecimen />

The library ships the **physics** — rim, recess, the raised signature action, the
lever with a throw, plate type — and the quiet components. It never ships a product's
hero object. Two mechanical gates hold the line: no material literal in any component
stylesheet, and no depth or plate type in a quiet one.

## Where to look

- **Foundations** — colour, type, space, radius, depth, motion, focus, breakpoints, code: as specimens, not tables.
- **Primitives · Controls** — the six Phase A components, every state, keyboard walkthroughs as tests.
- **Patterns** — the split, the dark panel in day, one signature action per screen.
- **Theming** — the contract a product fills, and all five reference themes on one composite.
- **Accessibility** — what is measured, what is gated, what is still by hand.
