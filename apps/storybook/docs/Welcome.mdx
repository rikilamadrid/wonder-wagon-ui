# Maker 047's bench

This Storybook is the **laboratory**: foundations as specimens, token visualisations,
component states and experiments. The system's story — which products use what, and the
public evidence for it — is the <a href="https://rikilamadrid.github.io/wonder-wagon-ui/" target="_top">Wonder Wagon Catalog</a>.

## What is public, and who uses it

Wonder Wagon is published as one React-free package, [`wonder-wagon-ui`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/wonder-wagon-ui):
the family doorway, the `wonder-wagon-ui/cli` terminal-identity generator, and static
tokens and themes at `wonder-wagon-ui/tokens/*` and `wonder-wagon-ui/themes/*`. The
`@wonder-wagon/tokens` and `@wonder-wagon/themes` workspaces are where those files are
authored; they aren't published separately.

Products use it to different depths:

- **Pathfinder** — its documentation site's semantic colour layer and its CLI identity. The deepest consumer.
- **Lorekeeper** — a generated family chrome layer (day/night toggle, serial plate) and its CLI identity. The rest of its site is its own `--lk-*` design.
- **Forge** — its CLI identity only. Its website is product-owned.
- **Lama** — draft and unreleased; consumes nothing yet.

## The components here are experimental

The React components in this Storybook have **no production consumers yet** and are not
published as a package. They're where component ideas are tested until a real product
needs one. They are not the design system.

## The one rule

Important things are objects with material, edge and thickness. Supporting things are
quiet paper and plain type. If a component appears more than three times on one
screen, it is quiet. Objects are singular.

<ObjectQuietSpecimen />

The library ships the **physics** — rim, recess, the raised signature action, the
lever with a throw, plate type — and the quiet components. It never ships a product's
hero object. Two mechanical gates hold the line: no material literal in any component
stylesheet, and no depth or plate type in a quiet one.

## Where to look

Every story sits in a **specimen tray** on the bench. The toolbar throws three levers:
the **environment** (day, night, auto), the **product theme** (the bench itself, or one
of the products), and the **ground** behind the specimen.

- **Foundations** — colour, type, space, radius, depth, motion, focus, breakpoints, code: as specimens, not tables.
- **Primitives · Controls** — the six experimental components, every state, keyboard walkthroughs as tests.
- **Patterns** — the split, the dark panel in day, one signature action per screen.
- **Theming** — the contract a product fills, and the reference themes on one composite.
- **Accessibility** — what is measured, what is gated, what is still by hand.
