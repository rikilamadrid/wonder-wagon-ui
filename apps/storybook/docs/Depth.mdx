# Depth

One light source, upper left. Cast shadows are black at low alpha; warmth comes from
the rim highlight and surface gradients. Every stack ships **whole** — the ten-layer
object well is one token — so nobody approximates it with `0 2px 4px` and quietly
flattens the system. The quiet card's shadow is the one permitted tinted exception,
named as such and confined to quiet cards.
